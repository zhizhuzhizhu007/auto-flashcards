2. Scope of Service & Features

Auto Flashcards is an independent third-party layout utility created for the Canva platform. Its primary features currently include:

  • Automated Flashcard Matrix Layout: Arranging vocabulary and text items into structured grid presets (2 × 3, 3 × 3, 4 × 4) with automatic pagination across multiple design pages.
  • Smart Multi-Delimiter Parsing: Intelligent parsing supporting line breaks, commas (English and Chinese), semicolons, and enumeration punctuation, with protection for compound phrases.
  • Print Hairline Cutting Guides: Optional generation of 0.5pt crop lines and 5mm cutting channels to aid in manual trimming of physical cards.
  • Item Safeguards: Real-time capacity counting and a 120-item guardrail to ensure smooth canvas rendering and prevent browser crashes.

Because Auto Flashcards operates as an embedded application inside Canva, its runtime availability and execution performance depend in part on Canva's platform infrastructure and SDK APIs.
