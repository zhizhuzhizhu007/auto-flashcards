Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

1. Overview & Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled in connection with Auto Flashcards ("we", "us", or "our"), which encompasses:

  1. The Auto Flashcards Canva Application: The design and layout utility running inside the Canva platform.
  2. The Auto Flashcards Website: The static informational site hosted on GitHub Pages (https://zhizhuzhizhu007.github.io/auto-flashcards/).

We are committed to operating with complete transparency. Auto Flashcards is an independent design utility operating strictly within the Canva Apps runtime and hosted via GitHub Pages. Our software architecture is intentionally engineered so that your vocabulary data, flashcard text, and designs never leave your local Canva browser session.

2. The Auto Flashcards Canva Application

2.1 Architecture and 100% Local Execution

Auto Flashcards is built using the official Canva Apps SDK. In its technical implementation:

  • Local Parsing & Layout Computation: All vocabulary string tokenization, multi-delimiter parsing, grid coordinate calculations, auto-wrapping, and page allocation are computed locally within your browser's JavaScript engine.
  • Zero External Data Collection or Remote Servers: Auto Flashcards does not maintain or connect to any external backend server, remote API, database, user tracking service, or advertising network. Your vocabulary lists, study content, and generated flashcards are never transmitted to us or any third party.
  • No Dedicated User Accounts: Auto Flashcards requires no separate account creation, email login, or passwords. It operates purely as an embedded extension inside your authorized Canva workspace.
  • Direct Canvas Insertion: Card boundary shapes, centered vocabulary text elements, and 0.5pt crop guide dividers are drawn directly onto your active Canva design pages using official Canva Apps SDK APIs.

2.2 Canva Permissions

The Auto Flashcards Canva application requests standard permissions specified in its application manifest:

  • canva:design:content:read: To inspect design page dimensions and structure for precise matrix layout and coordinate calculations.
  • canva:design:content:write: To create additional design pages as needed for multi-page pagination and to insert card elements, vocabulary text boxes, and crop guide lines onto your active canvas.

These permissions are exercised strictly and solely to deliver the flashcard generation features initiated by you when clicking the "Generate Flashcards" button.

2.3 Platform Telemetry & Infrastructure Notice

Important Platform Note:

While Auto Flashcards does not independently collect analytics or user telemetry, the application executes within Canva's environment. Canva may collect operational logs, diagnostic metrics, and usage statistics in accordance with Canva's own privacy terms. We recommend reviewing Canva's Privacy Policy for platform-level data handling practices.
