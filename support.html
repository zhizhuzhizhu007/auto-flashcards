Frequently Asked Questions

How does auto-pagination work across multiple pages?

Auto Flashcards calculates how many cards fit onto each page based on your selected preset (for example, 6 cards per page for 2 × 3). If your vocabulary list has 18 words, the app automatically populates the first 6 cards on your active page, generates 2 additional pages in your Canva design, and distributes the remaining cards in sequential order.

What delimiters are supported for separating words?

You can separate words using line breaks (Enter), standard English commas (,), Chinese full-width commas (，), semicolons (; or ；), or Chinese enumeration marks (、). Multi-word phrases such as "New York City" or "ice cream" are preserved as single items when separated by punctuation or lines.

Can I customize card borders, colors, and fonts after generation?

Yes! All generated cards and text boxes are inserted as standard native Canva elements. You can freely change fonts, sizes, text colors, background colors, and border strokes using Canva's native top toolbar at any time.

Why is there a limit of 120 items per generation?

To protect your browser from high memory consumption and avoid Canva API canvas transaction timeouts, Auto Flashcards enforces a 120-item safeguard. For larger decks (e.g. 300 words), simply generate them in 2–3 batches.

Do I need a separate user account or paid subscription?

No. Auto Flashcards operates directly inside your Canva editor without external registration, logins, or separate subscriptions.

Where can I track updates or review source documentation?

You can track project updates, report issues, and review releases on our public GitHub repository at github.com/zhizhuzhizhu007/auto-flashcards.
